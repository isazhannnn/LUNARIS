# Informal playtesting

**Reported by Parsec Studio: Meiirzhan Issa and Zhasulan Zholdas.**

In informal playtesting, our friends and acquaintances completed all four science missions and Alex's story without help from our team. When they were unsure how to proceed, they used PIX's in-game hints.

This feedback concerns completing the game and using its built-in guidance. Classroom learning outcomes have not yet been formally evaluated.
